# Validation Experiment Kit

ASSUMPTION: this is a proposed experiment, not market validation.

NOT RUN — no responses or results collected

## Hypothesis

ASSUMPTION: Entrepreneurs have recently struggled to turn an uncertain startup assumption into a concrete validation experiment

## Target Audience

Entrepreneurs investigating an early-stage digital product idea

## Customer Interview Guide

- Tell me about the last time you encountered this problem. What happened?
- What steps did you take to address it that time?
- Which tools or alternatives did you use, and how did they fit your process?
- What time or effort did that experience require?
- What was difficult about your existing approach?
- When did this last occur before that occasion?
- What, if anything, have you already tried to change about this process?

## Validation Experiment

Invite consenting adults from this segment: Entrepreneurs investigating an early-stage digital product idea. Investigate this problem without pitching a solution: Founders may struggle to distinguish supported claims from assumptions and choose a practical next validation experiment. Conduct voluntary interviews about recent actual behavior. Explain the research purpose; allow skipping any question or withdrawing. Keep only anonymous aggregate notes outside this prototype; do not collect identifying or sensitive information. No outreach is automated.

## Success Criteria

PROPOSED ASSUMPTION — requires founder approval: 3 of 5 participants. Participants describing a recent concrete instance and an existing workaround

## Failure Criteria

If fewer participants meet the approved criterion, the hypothesis is weakened. Repeated absence of the problem or satisfactory existing alternatives also weakens it. An incomplete or biased sample is inconclusive; this kit makes no business gate decision.

## Recommended Next Steps

- Review the hypothesis, audience, constraints and interview guide before recruitment.
- Approve or revise the proposed sample size and success threshold before collecting results; freeze the approved version.
- Recruit voluntarily through channels you are permitted to use; do not send messages from this tool.
- Ask about past behavior before showing any concept. Record anonymous observations, including contradictory observations.
- Compare collected observations to the approved criteria and return real evidence to the research workflow.

## Founder Constraints

- Hackathon prototype with two developers
- No paid AI services or payments
- Local artifacts only; founder approval before artifact generation
